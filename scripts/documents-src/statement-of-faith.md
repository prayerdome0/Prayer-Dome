# Prayer Dome Statement of Faith

A clear summary of what Prayer Dome believes, teaches and lives by — held in common by every member, leader and serving team across Zambia, Eswatini, Ireland and beyond.

## Introduction

At Prayer Dome we gather as a house of prayer for all nations, rooted in the historic Christian faith and the authority of Scripture. This statement does not replace the Bible; it summarises what we understand the Bible to teach and how we seek to live it together.

## 1. The Bible — God’s Inspired Word

We believe the Bible — sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments — is God’s inspired, truthful and authoritative Word. It was carried by human authors under the guidance of the Holy Spirit, without error in what God intended to teach, and it remains sufficient for doctrine, correction, training in righteousness and every good work. We receive it with reverence, read it daily, study it carefully, obey it humbly and teach it faithfully.

- The Bible reveals who God is, who humanity is, what has gone wrong through sin, and what God has done in Christ to restore us.
- No experience, tradition or revelation that contradicts Scripture is accepted as authoritative.
- Every teaching at Prayer Dome is tested against Scripture in community, with prayer for wisdom.

*Key texts: 2 Timothy 3:16-17, Psalm 119:105, 2 Peter 1:20-21, Hebrews 4:12*

## 2. God — Father, Son and Holy Spirit

We believe in one living and true God, eternal in three persons — Father, Son and Holy Spirit — equal in nature, distinct in person, perfect in holiness, wisdom, power, justice and love. God created all things visible and invisible, sustains them moment by moment, and reigns sovereignly over history for His glory and our good.

- **Father:** creator, provider and judge who so loved the world that He sent His Son.
- **Son:** our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom and for whom all things were made.
- **Holy Spirit:** the Lord who proceeds from the Father and the Son, who convicts, regenerates, indwells, seals and empowers.

*Key texts: Deuteronomy 6:4, Matthew 28:19, John 1:1-3, Colossians 1:16-17*

## 3. Jesus Christ — Fully God and Fully Human

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully human, one person with two natures. He was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the virgin Mary, lived without sin, performed signs and wonders confirming His ministry, taught with heaven’s authority, died on the cross as a substitutionary atonement for our sins, rose bodily from the dead on the third day, ascended to heaven, is seated at the right hand of the Father, and will return personally and visibly to judge the living and the dead.

His perfect life, atoning death and victorious resurrection are the sole basis for our reconciliation with God. There is no other name by which we must be saved.

*Key texts: John 1:14, Philippians 2:5-11, Romans 5:8, 1 Corinthians 15:3-4, Acts 1:11*

## 4. Salvation — By Grace Through Faith

We believe salvation is a gift of God’s grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Human effort, heritage, good works and religious observance cannot earn it. When a person repents and believes:

- Their sins are forgiven and guilt is removed.
- They are justified — declared righteous before God through Christ’s righteousness credited to them.
- They are born again — made new by the Holy Spirit and adopted into God’s family.
- They receive eternal life, beginning now and fulfilled in resurrection glory.

Salvation produces a changed life: ongoing repentance, obedience to Jesus, growth in holiness, love for the church and a witness to the world. Assurance rests not in our strength but in Christ’s finished work and the Spirit’s testimony.

*Key texts: Ephesians 2:8-10, Titus 3:5, Romans 8:15-16, John 10:27-28*

## 5. The Holy Spirit — Our Helper and Transforming Presence

We believe the Holy Spirit indwells every believer from the moment of faith, seals them for the day of redemption, empowers prayer and witness, guides into truth, convicts of sin, produces Christlike character, grants spiritual gifts, and builds the church. We welcome His leading, testing what claims to be His work by Scripture, character and community discernment.

We seek to be filled with the Spirit — not as a single event only, but as an ongoing walk — expressed in worship, prayerfulness, bold witness, humility, purity and service. Gifts are given to serve others, exercised in love and order.

*Key texts: John 14:16-17, Acts 1:8, Galatians 5:22-23, 1 Corinthians 12:7, Ephesians 5:18*

## 6. The Church — The People of God

We believe the church is the global and local assembly of those called out by the gospel — a family, a temple, a body and a royal priesthood. We are called to:

- Worship the Triune God reverently and joyfully.
- Teach and guard sound doctrine and encourage faithful living.
- Share fellowship, break bread, pray together and bear one another’s burdens.
- Practice baptism for believers and the Lord’s Supper as commanded by Christ.
- Equip every member for ministry and send workers for mission.
- Care for the poor, the vulnerable, the lonely and the next generation.

Prayer Dome expresses this locally through weekly worship, prayer watches, small groups, service teams and pastoral care, and globally through prayer, partnership and shared mission.

*Key texts: Matthew 16:18, Acts 2:42-47, 1 Corinthians 12:12-27, 1 Peter 2:9*

## 7. Christian Living — A Life Worthy of the Gospel

We believe those who have received grace are called to live worthy of it:

- **Love:** supreme love for God and neighbour, reflecting God’s character.
- **Holiness:** turning from sin by grace, pursuing purity in heart, speech, body and relationships.
- **Forgiveness:** forgiving as we have been forgiven, seeking reconciliation where possible and keeping healthy boundaries where needed.
- **Integrity:** truthfulness, financial faithfulness, sexual purity, respect for authority and care for creation.
- **Service:** humble, willing contribution of time, gifts and resources for the body and community.
- **Mission:** personal evangelism, compassionate action and support for gospel work locally and among the nations.

We do not pursue these things to earn favour, but because favour has been given freely in Christ.

*Key texts: Matthew 5-7, Colossians 3:1-17, Micah 6:8, James 1:27*

## 8. Marriage, Family and Human Dignity

We believe every person is created in God’s image, equal in dignity and worthy of respect, protection and kindness. We honour life from conception, uphold marriage as the lifelong union of one man and one woman, and receive children as a gift to be nurtured in faith and safety. Our pastoral posture to every person — whatever their story — is truth spoken in love, grace without compromise, and commitment to walk alongside people toward holiness and wholeness.

*Key texts: Genesis 1:27, Psalm 139:13-16, Mark 10:6-9, Ephesians 5:21-33*

## 9. The Ordinances and Practices

We practice believer’s baptism by immersion as a public identification with Christ, and the Lord’s Supper as a remembrance of His death, thanksgiving for His resurrection, and anticipation of His return. We devote ourselves to prayer — together and alone, morning and evening — building a rhythm of praise, thanksgiving, confession, petition, intercession and listening for the Spirit through Scripture.

## 10. Last Things — The Return of Christ and Eternal Hope

We believe Jesus Christ will return bodily, visibly and gloriously. He will raise the dead — believers to everlasting life in the new heavens and new earth, where righteousness dwells, and unbelievers to judgment. Until that day we live between cross and crown: praying “Your kingdom come,” longing for Christ’s appearing, labouring in hope, and proclaiming the gospel so that many more are ready.

Because our hope is sure, we do not despair in suffering; we worship through trials and serve with urgency and joy.

*Key texts: John 14:1-3, 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18, Revelation 21:1-5, Mark 7:37 — “He hath done all things well.”*
