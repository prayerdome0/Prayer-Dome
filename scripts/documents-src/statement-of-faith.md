# Prayer Dome Statement of Faith

## The Bible
We believe the Bible is God’s inspired, reliable and authoritative Word, useful for teaching, correction, training and equipping believers for every good work.

## God
We believe in one God—Father, Son and Holy Spirit—who created all things and reigns with perfect wisdom, holiness, power and love.

## Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully human. He was born of the virgin Mary, lived without sin, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to heaven and will return.

## Salvation
We believe salvation is a gift of grace received through faith in Jesus Christ. No human effort can earn salvation. Believers are forgiven, made new and called to live as disciples.

## The Holy Spirit
We believe the Holy Spirit indwells believers, empowers prayer and witness, produces Christlike character, and distributes gifts for the edification of the church.

## The Church
We believe the church is the global and local body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, teaching, prayer, communion, baptism, service and mission.

## Christian Living
We believe believers are called to love God, love neighbors, pursue holiness, practice forgiveness, serve generously, share the gospel and care for the vulnerable.

## Eternal Hope
We believe Jesus Christ will return, believers will be raised to eternal life, and God’s kingdom will be fully established.
