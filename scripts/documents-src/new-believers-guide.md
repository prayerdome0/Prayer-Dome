# New Believer’s Growth Guide

Welcome to the family of God. Your decision to follow Jesus is the most important decision you will ever make.

## 1. Begin with Prayer
Pray simply and honestly each day. Thank God, worship Him, confess sin, bring your requests, and pray for others. Use the Lord’s Prayer as a pattern in Matthew 6:9–13.

## 2. Read the Bible Daily
Start with the Gospel of John, then Romans, Psalms and Proverbs. Ask:
- What does this teach me about God?
- What does it teach about people and sin?
- What promise or command should I obey?
- Who can I share this with?

## 3. Join a Church Family
Faith is personal but not private. Attend worship, join a small group, serve on a team and build accountable relationships.

## 4. Practice Obedience
Be baptized, obey Jesus’ teachings, forgive others, control your speech, honor God with your finances and live with integrity.

## 5. Share Your Story
Write a short testimony: what your life was like before Christ, how you met Jesus, and what He is doing now.

## 6. Serve
Every believer has gifts. Ask God where He wants you to serve. Faithful service in small things prepares you for greater responsibility.

## 7. Depend on the Holy Spirit
Ask the Holy Spirit to guide, strengthen and produce His fruit in you: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control.
