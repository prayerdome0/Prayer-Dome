About these translations — please read The English is the King James Version and is exact. The Chitumbuka and siSwati renderings are Prayer Dome community drafts, prepared as a starting point and marked awaiting review until a fluent speaker has checked each one. They are not the published Buku Lakupatulika or siSwati Bible and should not be quoted as an official translation. If you speak either language and can help us correct them, please get in touch — it would be a real gift to the church.
