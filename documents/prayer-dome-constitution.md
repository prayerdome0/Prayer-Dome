# Prayer Dome Constitution

**Edition:** 2026  
**Motto:** A House of Prayer for All Nations  
**Theme Scripture:** Mark 7:37 — “He hath done all things well.”

## 1. Name and Purpose
The ministry shall be known as **Prayer Dome**. It exists to exalt Jesus Christ, teach the Word of God, promote persistent prayer, disciple believers, support missions, and serve communities with compassion and integrity.

## 2. Statement of Faith
Prayer Dome believes in:
- The divine inspiration and authority of the Holy Bible.
- One God eternally existing in Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
- The virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection and second coming of Jesus Christ.
- Salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
- The work of the Holy Spirit in convicting, regenerating, empowering and gifting believers.
- The local church as Christ’s body for worship, fellowship, discipleship, service and mission.

## 3. Membership
Members are believers who confess Jesus Christ as Lord, agree with the statement of faith, live in Christian obedience, and commit to prayer, faithful giving, fellowship and service. Membership is open to all nations, tribes and languages, reflecting the prayer-house vision of Isaiah 56:7.

## 4. Leadership
Leadership shall include pastoral oversight, ministry team leaders, trustees or administrative officers, and servants called to specific functions. Leaders must model humility, accountability, sound doctrine, financial integrity and faithful prayer.

## 5. Meetings
Prayer Dome shall gather for worship, prayer, teaching, communion, baptism and outreach as arranged by leadership. Members are encouraged to participate in small groups, prayer watches and serving teams.

## 6. Finances
Funds shall be received through tithes, offerings, gifts and approved fundraising. All resources shall be used for ministry purposes, including outreach, staff support, facility costs, missions, benevolence and technology. Financial records shall be reviewed and reported responsibly.

## 7. Conduct and Discipline
Members and leaders shall pursue holiness, honesty, reconciliation and love. Matters of serious disagreement should first be addressed through biblical reconciliation. Church discipline, when necessary, shall be redemptive, confidential where possible and aimed at restoration.

## 8. Amendments
This constitution may be amended by leadership approval after prayer, consultation and clear communication. Amendments must remain consistent with Scripture and the stated purpose of Prayer Dome.
