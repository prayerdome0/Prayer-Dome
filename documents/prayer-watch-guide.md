# Prayer Watch Guide

## Why We Pray
Prayer is both fellowship with God and spiritual warfare. Jesus often prayed before major decisions and taught His disciples to persist in prayer.

## A Simple Prayer Pattern
1. **Praise** — Worship God for who He is.
2. **Thanksgiving** — Thank Him for salvation, provision and mercy.
3. **Confession** — Ask forgiveness and receive cleansing.
4. **Petition** — Bring personal needs before God.
5. **Intercession** — Pray for family, church, leaders, nations, the sick and the lost.
6. **Listening** — Pause and ask the Holy Spirit to guide you through Scripture.

## Prayer Points
- Families, marriages and children
- Salvation of the lost
- Pastors, leaders and serving teams
- Revival in Zambia, Eswatini, Ireland and the nations
- Healing, deliverance and provision
- Protection over children, youth and members
- Wisdom in decisions and resources

## Practical Safeguards
- Pray with Scripture, not superstition.
- Avoid condemnation when prayer feels difficult.
- Balance night prayer with rest and health.
- Pray in agreement with other believers when possible.
- Keep a prayer journal for answered requests.
