# Small Group Discussion Guide

## Purpose
Small groups help believers grow through Bible study, fellowship, prayer, accountability and mission.

## Meeting Pattern
1. Welcome and worship — 10 minutes
2. Check-in — 10 minutes
3. Lesson or Scripture discussion — 30 minutes
4. Prayer and care — 20 minutes
5. Outreach planning — 10 minutes

## Discussion Questions
- What stood out from the lesson or passage?
- What did you learn about God, yourself and others?
- Where is obedience required?
- Who needs help, follow-up or salvation?
- What will we do before the next meeting?

## Group Covenant
- Keep confidentiality unless harm or danger requires disclosure.
- Listen without interrupting.
- Speak truth in love.
- Pray for one another during the week.
- Invite visitors and new members.
- Serve together in one outreach or ministry project each term.
