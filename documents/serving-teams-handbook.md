# Serving Teams Handbook

## Serving Values
Every team exists to glorify God and help people encounter Jesus. Serve with humility, excellence, punctuality, confidentiality and teamwork.

## Team Expectations
- Maintain a personal life of prayer and Bible study.
- Arrive prepared and on time.
- Follow leadership instructions.
- Speak respectfully to members, visitors and fellow volunteers.
- Protect the dignity and privacy of people you serve.
- Report problems quickly and avoid gossip.
- Care for equipment, rooms and digital systems.

## Team Areas
- **Prayer Team:** prays with members, maintains altars and covers services.
- **Worship Team:** leads the congregation into God’s presence.
- **Media/Tech Team:** manages sound, streaming, slides and online platforms.
- **Ushering/Hospitality:** welcomes people, organizes seating and assists visitors.
- **Children/Youth:** teaches, protects and disciples young people.
- **Follow-up:** contacts new believers, first-time guests and small-group members.
- **Administration:** supports records, schedules, communication and resources.

## Growth Steps
Attend team meetings, complete relevant Academy lessons, ask for feedback, and train others as you grow.
