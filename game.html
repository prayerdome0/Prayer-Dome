Warming up the 3D stage…

Domey is the Prayer Dome 3D host, a stylised cartoon human who blinks, looks at you and moves his head and body as he talks, with lips that move through each word he says, a head that turns to you, and ears that hear your voice. Tap Start the show, then simply talk to him: say your answer, ask him to repeat, ask for a hint, or say skip. He replies out loud and explains the scripture behind every question.
