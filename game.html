Talk with Domey

Warming up the 3D stage…

Domey is the Prayer Dome 3D hostess: a friendly woman in royal blue and gold who blinks, looks at you and moves naturally while she talks — her lips follow every word she says. Answer by talking, by tapping A, B, C or D, or by typing. Ask her to repeat, ask for a hint, or say skip. She replies out loud and explains the scripture behind every question, and the whole conversation is written below so you can read along.

Domey appears instantly as a character modelled in code, then upgrades on capable devices to a licensed, rigged human model with real facial expressions and hand gestures.
