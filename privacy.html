Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Prayer Dome Ministry (“Prayer Dome”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a prayer, Bible, devotional, live-service, and Christian community platform at prayerdome.net and through the Prayer Dome Android app.

This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

If you create an account or sign in, we may receive your email address, display name, profile photo, authentication provider, and account identifier from Firebase Authentication.

Community content

When you use the prayer wall, chat, testimonies, comments, events, notes, or support features, we process the content and metadata you choose to submit. Uploaded images or media may be stored with our configured media providers.

Technical and usage information

The app may store preferences, saved Bible progress, language, theme, and notification state locally on your device. Firebase and hosting providers may process standard technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Location

Some features may request optional device location to show local context. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings. We do not require location to read prayers, Bible content, or devotionals.

How we use information

  • To provide authentication, account, community, Bible, devotional, live, and support features.
  • To display content you choose to share with the relevant community or ministry team.
  • To send notifications when you enable them and to maintain app preferences.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use sensitive personal information for targeted advertising.

Service providers

Prayer Dome relies on service providers including Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, and related infrastructure), hosting/CDN providers, Cloudinary for configured media features, and external Bible, location, currency, or media services when a feature requests them. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention, security, and your choices

We retain account and community data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet ministry or legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our rules. You may stop optional location or notification access through Android settings. You can request correction or deletion of your account data by contacting us; some records may need to be retained where legally required or needed for security.

No internet service is completely secure. Please do not post information in a public prayer, chat, or testimony that you do not want other users to see.

Children

Prayer Dome is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to info@prayerdome.net.
