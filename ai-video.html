How this works: the browser speaks the script aloud while the video renders. Browsers do not let a page grab that audio silently, so when you press Render, Chrome/Edge will ask you to share this tab — tick “Also share tab audio” and the narration is captured into the video. If you decline, the video still renders beautifully, just silent.
